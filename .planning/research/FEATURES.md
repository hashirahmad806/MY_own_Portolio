# Feature Research

**Domain:** High-End Creative Portfolio (Gorden Koschel / gravity)
**Researched:** 2026-10-06
**Confidence:** HIGH

## Core & Interactive Features

### 1. Immersive Editorial Dark Aesthetic
- Rich near-black color scheme (`#080808` / `#0f0f10`), warm ivory text (`#f5f5f3`), muted titanium and gold accents (`#c5a059` / `#888888`).
- Subtle procedural SVG noise/grain overlay creating a tactile luxury physical print sensation.
- Fluid typography utilizing bespoke font pairing (Modern Editorial Serif for high-impact titles + Precision Swiss Grotesk for body copy and metadata).

### 2. "Click to Gravitize" Physics Microinteraction
- Direct visual tribute to line 9-11 of `PORTFOLIO.MD`: `945, 592 a 0.4 1003, 575 a 1.5 [gravıty] click to gravitize`.
- On click or hover, trigger dynamic kinetic particle / celestial coordinate pull or letter-stagger distortion that simulates gravitational collapse and snapping back into orbital equilibrium.

### 3. Kinetic Typography & Scroll-Linked Text Scrubbing
- **Manifesto Scrubbing:** As the user scrolls through the manifesto ("Agentur ohne Agentur. 35 Jahre Erfahrung. Kein Overhead..."), words illuminate line by line from dim gray to luminous white.
- **Section Headers:** Split-character and split-line reveals using GSAP stagger timelines.

### 4. Hybrid AI / Agentic By Design Showcase
- Visual presentation of the human-AI hybrid philosophy: "Mensch und KI, jeder mit seinen Stärken. Lokale Modelle, diskrete Workflows, ungebändigte Kreativität."
- Interactive comparison/cards displaying the transformation: from bloated agency overhead to agile, agentive creative power.

### 5. Blue-Chip Client Roster & Infinite Velocity Ticker
- Prominent showcase of elite brands: Bosch, Vorwerk, Telefónica, HOCHTIEF, Gewobag, HILTI, thyssenkrupp, MLP, Giesecke+Devrient, BYK-Chemie, BYK-Gardner, Sarstedt, Fraport, Porsche.
- Seamless dual-direction interactive marquee ticker that reacts to scroll velocity and mouse drags.

### 6. Prestige Awards Vault
- Showcase of major industry honours:
  - Red Dot Grand Prix 2025 & "Best of the Best" (Telefónica Deutschland)
  - ADC-Wettbewerb 2023 & 2025
  - German Design Award Gold 2023 & 2024
  - German Brand Award 2023
  - iF Design Award 2023
  - BCM Gold 2023, Deutscher Digital Award, DDC, Deutscher PR-Preis
- Interactive award items with hover reveal for category tags, year, and direct links.

### 7. High-Converting Chemistry Meeting Call to Action
- Prominent magnetic action button with hover distortion: "Let’s meet up! Chemistry-Meeting" linking to `mailto:losgehts@gravity-design.de?subject=Chemistry-Meeting`.
- Smooth scroll-to-top button and clean semantic footer with legal/copyright notices.

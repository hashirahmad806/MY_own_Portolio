# gravity — Gorden Koschel Portfolio

## What This Is

An agency-grade, luxury editorial interactive portfolio for **Gorden Koschel** (Visual Strategist & Creative Visualizer / *gravity*), designed to showcase 35 years of creative direction excellence, high-profile German client engagements (Porsche, Bosch, Telefónica, Hilti), top design awards (Red Dot Grand Prix 2025, German Design Award Gold, ADC), and a pioneering hybrid human + AI creative workflow. Built with Astro, Lenis smooth scrolling, and GSAP kinetic animations.

## Core Value

Deliver an unforgettable, visually stunning, and butter-smooth digital experience that reflects Gorden Koschel's 35-year creative caliber and positions *gravity* as an elite, high-agency partner for forward-looking brands.

## Business Context

- **Audience**: High-profile brand leaders, corporate marketing directors, CMOs, creative agencies, and editorial decision-makers seeking senior visual strategy, branding, reports, and digital worlds.
- **Goal**: Convert visits into "Chemistry Meetings" via direct inquiry and establish undeniable authority in visual strategy, agentic AI-augmented workflows, and award-winning design.

## Technical Architecture & Brand System

- **Static Site Generator**: Astro (zero-JS default, island architecture, instant page loads)
- **Smooth Scrolling**: Lenis (`lenis`) for luxury inertial scrolling
- **Animation Framework**: GSAP (`gsap` + `ScrollTrigger`) for kinetic typography, scrubbed reveals, and microinteractions
- **Brand Palette & Tokens**:
  - Background: `--bg: #101010` (deep obsidian)
  - Foreground: `--fg: #EDEBE6` (warm ivory)
  - Accent: `--accent: #B481F8` (electric lilac / celestial gravity purple)
  - Overlay tints: `rgba(16,16,16,0.72)` / `rgba(16,16,16,0.38)`
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS3 design system with custom properties, typography tokens, procedural grain textures, and dark editorial aesthetic
- **Deployment & SEO**: Static generation, semantic HTML5, OpenGraph tags, JSON-LD schema (ProfessionalService & Person), perfect Lighthouse metrics
- **Live Reference**: [gravity-design.de](https://gravity-design.de/)

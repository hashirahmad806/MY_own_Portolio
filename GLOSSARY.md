# Gravity Portfolio

A 100% pixel-perfect and animation-identical replica of the luxury editorial portfolio for Gorden Koschel (gravity-design.de).

## Language

**Stage**:
The fixed 1920×1080 celestial physics coordinate viewport framing the hero brand statement, Newtonian gravitational collapse, and orbital telemetry readouts.
_Avoid_: Hero banner, splash screen

**Orbit World**:
The dual-layer 3D CSS perspective universe (`#orbitBack` and `#orbitFront`) presenting case study cards with interactive drag physics, momentum inertia, and Heureka/AI classification badges.
_Avoid_: Carousel, project slider

**Bold Curve**:
The organic quadratic Bezier SVG curve transitioning from dark obsidian space into the full-bleed lilac manifesto section.
_Avoid_: Section divider, curved wave

**Manifesto Ring**:
The rotating celestial orbital ring and revolving satellite dot that frames the manifesto's typography.
_Avoid_: Spinner, loader

**Constellation Plexus**:
The multi-tiered interactive SVG orbital network illustrating local AI model agents and collaborative human-AI nodes.
_Avoid_: Spider graph, network diagram

**Reading Slot**:
A generous vertical scroll block within the 3-column sticky bio grid that reveals and illuminates typographic characters sequentially during scrolling.
_Avoid_: Article block, paragraph container

**Contact Orb**:
The magnetic floating sphere that ascends through a parabolic trajectory (`bahn(g)`) out of a curvature depression mask to invite Chemistry Meetings.
_Avoid_: Contact bubble, submit button

**Blend Footer**:
The fixed site footer utilizing `mix-blend-mode: difference` that appears at the top and bottom of the page with a pulsing vertical tick line.
_Avoid_: Bottom bar, copyright footer

**Parametric Telemetry**:
The real-time coordinate HUD and orbital curve guides calculated via harmonic trigonometric functions tracking focal distances and orbital angles.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, stats counter

**Active Hemisphere**:
The foreground volume of the 3D Orbit Universe where $Z \ge 0$, within which project video loops are actively streamed and card interactions are prioritized.
_Avoid_: Front layer, visible cards

**Impressum**:
The dedicated legal notice and data protection disclosure page required under German telemedia law, served statically at `/impressum`.
_Avoid_: About page, legal modal
